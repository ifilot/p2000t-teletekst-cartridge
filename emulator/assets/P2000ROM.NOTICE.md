# P2000T monitor ROM

`P2000ROM.bin` is the 4096-byte Philips P2000T monitor image distributed with
the upstream [p2000t/M2000](https://github.com/p2000t/M2000) emulator. It is
bundled here because the cartridge emulator cannot boot without it.

- Upstream path: `P2000ROM.bin` on the M2000 `develop` branch
- SHA-1: `4dbb28adad30587f2ea536ba116898d459faccac`
- SHA-256: `351e0d3dcb9e39e0ed375bb0cb7debf7a6e8afcc93a3aa1f147dfdf68392dac8`

The ROM is historical firmware and is not covered by the emulator's GPL-3.0
license.
